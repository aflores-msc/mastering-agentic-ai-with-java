Refund and Return Policy

Return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they
are in their original condition. After 30 days, returns are not accepted.

Defective items
If an item arrives defective or damaged, the customer is entitled to a full refund or a
free replacement, even beyond the standard 30 day window, up to 90 days from delivery.
The customer should report the defect and may be asked to share a photo.

Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable software, and personal care items that have been opened cannot
be returned.

How refunds are issued
Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
