Shipping and Warranty Policy

Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
Orders placed after 6 PM are processed the next business day.

Tracking
A tracking link is emailed once an order is shipped. An order marked IN_TRANSIT is on its
way and has not been delivered yet.

Warranty
Electronics come with a 1 year manufacturer warranty covering manufacturing defects.
The warranty does not cover accidental damage or normal wear and tear.

Lost packages
If a package is marked delivered but not received, contact support within 7 days so we can
open an investigation with the courier.
