Telusko AI Course - Syllabus Overview

Part 1: Foundations
AI and machine learning basics, embeddings, tokenization, and how large language models work.

Part 2: Spring AI
ChatModel and ChatClient, chat memory, tool calling, structured output, and Retrieval
Augmented Generation with vector databases such as PGVector and Redis.

Part 3: LangChain4j
The low-level ChatModel API, AI Services in Spring Boot, streaming, structured output,
tool calling, guardrails, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Part 4: Agentic AI
Multi-agent workflows (sequential, loop, parallel, conditional), supervisor agents that
plan on their own, agents that use real tools, and Retrieval Augmented Generation with
LangChain4j.

Capstone project
Students build one complete AI powered application of their own choosing and submit it for
review. The three strongest projects receive a free Telusko live course of the author's
choice.
