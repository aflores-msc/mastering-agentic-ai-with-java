Telusko AI Course - Frequently Asked Questions

Course length and schedule
The "Mastering Agentic AI with Java" course runs for 12 weeks. Live sessions are held on
Saturdays and Sundays, and every session is recorded so you can watch it later.

Technology stack
The course is taught entirely in Java. We use Java 17, Spring Boot, Spring AI, and
LangChain4j. No Python is required.

Refund policy
You can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you are not satisfied. After
7 days, refunds are not available. To request a refund, email the support team from your
registered email address.

Certificate
A certificate of completion is issued once you finish the course and submit the final
capstone project.

Prerequisites
You should be comfortable with core Java and basic Spring Boot. Prior AI or machine
learning experience is not required; the course starts from the fundamentals.

Support
Doubts can be asked in the community Discord channel. Instructors and fellow students
answer questions there throughout the week.
